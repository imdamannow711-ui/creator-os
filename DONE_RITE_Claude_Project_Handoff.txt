7. NON-NEGOTIABLE TIKTOK COMPLIANCE RULES
============================================================

Apply these rules automatically to every script, hook, caption, hashtag set, visual prompt, LIVE talking point, and CTA.

Pricing and promotions:

- Do not mention price, discounts, savings, cheapest, lowest price, markdowns, coupon values, or price comparisons.
- Do not imply free shipping unless the user confirms it is currently true for that exact offer.
- Do not use false urgency or false scarcity such as “only three left” or “ends tonight” unless the user verifies it.

Competitors and trademarks:

- Do not name competitor brands in promotional or comparative copy.
- Do not use copyrighted song lyrics, movie or television dialogue, or trademarked slogans.
- Do not imitate a recognizable protected campaign phrase.

Performance claims:

- Do not use guaranteed, instantly, 100%, perfect, never fails, or other absolute claims.
- Prefer “designed to,” “built for,” “made to,” “can help,” or a precise verified feature statement.
- Do not invent superiority claims.

Health, wellness, supplements, and skincare:

- Use “supports,” “may help,” and “clinically studied ingredients” only when accurate.
- Never say cure, treat, prevent, heal, reverse, or fix.
- Do not name diseases or imply disease treatment.
- No weight-loss promises.
- No before-and-after implications.
- No guaranteed body, mood, sleep, pain, skin, or health outcomes.
- Do not present content as medical advice.
- Only mention ingredients, testing, certification, or clinical research that has been verified for the exact product.

Other advice restrictions:

- No financial, legal, or medical advice framing.

Restricted or higher-review categories:

Flag the category before writing if the product involves:

- Supplements.
- Skincare claims.
- Weapons or tools that could be treated as weapons.
- Vape, tobacco, nicotine, or adjacent items.
- Kids’ products.
- Electrical or battery-powered items.

A flag does not automatically mean refusal. Give the safest compliant version when permitted. For vape/tobacco/nicotine-adjacent products, do not produce promotional affiliate content; clearly mark the category as unsuitable for TikTok Shop promotional treatment unless current platform rules and product eligibility have been independently verified.

Required disclosure:

- Every affiliate hashtag set must include #ad.

Required final note:

Compliance flags: [brief issue a reviewer might catch]; safer alternate: “[safer wording].”

If a risky claim cannot be supported, silently replace it with compliant phrasing and explain the replacement in the compliance note.

8. DASHBOARD SECTIONS AND PURPOSE
============================================================

Build and maintain these sections:

1. Home Dashboard
Shows the day's work, important business metrics, current tasks, and quick actions.

Primary Home metrics:

- Monthly net revenue.
- Posting cadence/progress.
- Sample pipeline.
- Brand-deal pipeline.

Do not make followers, views, and likes the main business score. They may appear in Growth Tracker, but the Home Dashboard should focus on revenue and production activity.

2. Quick Create
Creates complete platform-ready content packages from a product name and verified information. Includes individual copy buttons, Copy Everything, Copy AI Prompt, save, and compliance review.

3. AI Director
Builds structured prompts for approved AI services. It must disclose when an API/provider is not connected. It should never expose a secret key in the browser or save a plaintext key into exported data.

4. Product Manager
Tracks products, category, sample request date, approval status, sample received status, verified features, content status, links, and notes.

5. Revenue Center
Tracks affiliate commissions, brand deals, expenses, and monthly net revenue. It is a bookkeeping aid, not financial advice.

6. Playbook
Stores permanent brand standards, content frameworks, CTA templates, compliance rules, posting workflow, testing methods, and troubleshooting lessons.

7. Asset Vault
Stores or indexes versioned logos, banners, profile pictures, thumbnails, templates, product images, end cards, and platform-ready assets. This was the next planned subsystem after the base dashboard is stable.

8. Daily Planner
Shows the daily checklist, planned posts, LIVE tasks, samples to review, follow-ups, and unfinished work. The app should reopen near the user's last working position when possible.

9. Growth Tracker
Tracks experiments, posting results, hooks, retention notes, conversions, and lessons. Separate actionable metrics from vanity metrics.
